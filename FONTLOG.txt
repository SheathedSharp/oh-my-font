0.400 — approved complete redesign
The owner approved the two completed reference cuts in PR #10 and authorized
publication. Official release: LihuiT Regular and zayJu Regular, weight 400,
810 codepoints and 986 glyphs each. No old 0.301 outlines, synthetic weights or
Obliques are included. Accepted curves/metrics/layout are unchanged by release
identity promotion. Improved accent-aware features, ogonek attachment, ligature
carets and cross-format joins. OFL and original-source attribution retained.

0.301 — first public release
- Rename Lihui to LihuiT and Zixian to zayJu; preserve author zayju.
- Repair naming, code-page/style flags, underline metrics, STAT linking and
  unhinted scan control. No new outline redesign is claimed.
- Apply OFL-1.1 and preserve original-source attribution in metadata/packages.
- Add desktop/web distribution packages and an interactive real-font specimen.
- Record exact-artifact checks and known limited-coverage/typographic findings.

0.300 — owner-supplied engineering baseline
- Reference-led Latin reconstruction, paired text/display cuts, eight weights
  and upright/Oblique, from geometry and 19 reference-derived glyphs.
