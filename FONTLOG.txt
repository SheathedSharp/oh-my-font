0.302 — development candidate (unreleased)
Independent per-weight source masters and junction corrections; real CFF/TrueType
curves, source isolation and full outline/renderer verification. Drawings are
migrated and repaired original artwork, not a claim of a complete fresh hand
redraw. Existing OFL, author and original-source attribution remain unchanged.

0.301 — first public release
- Rename Lihui to LihuiT and Zixian to zayJu; preserve author zayju.
- Repair naming, code-page/style flags, underline metrics, STAT linking and
  unhinted scan control. No new outline redesign is claimed.
- Apply OFL-1.1 and preserve original-source attribution in metadata/packages.
- Add desktop/web distribution packages and an interactive real-font specimen.
- Record exact-artifact checks and known limited-coverage/typographic findings.

  and upright/Oblique, from geometry and 19 reference-derived glyphs.
