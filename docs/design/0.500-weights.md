# 0.500 weight masters

Regular 400 upright is the owner-approved 0.400 drawing. It is not in the tables below and it is not retargeted. Every other upright weight is a stored UFO. The numbers are hand-set per family. They are not one stem delta, and they do not lie on a straight line from Thin to Black.

Reference stems, measured on H/I of the approved Regular: LihuiT vertical 89 / horizontal 74; zayJu vertical 102 / horizontal 86. Units are UPM 1000.

The stroke pass measures a local ray. A hit is treated as a stroke only when it is under 2.35× the Regular stem; otherwise the point moves by that weight’s stem delta, blended toward the join target at sharp turns. Counters move by `counter_move`, not by the outer delta. Review letters (B, M, R, a, g, e, 2, 3, 6, 8, 9) get their own bowl, waist, valley, bar, aperture and counter edits before and after that pass. A last-resort elliptical buffer is not used on those letters.

**100.** 竖茎约 34，横画仍留 26，接合 48 重于茎，避免尖脚断开。B 的上碗仍小于下碗，腰部松开；M 的谷抬高，避免细线收成针尖。e 的横画上移、开口加大。字距明显放松。

**300.** 不是细体到 Regular 的中点。茎 58/48，比按 100→400 线性插值更轻，对比开始收回。B 的腰几乎回到参考，M 只轻轻抬谷。字距只加一点空气。

**500.** 竖茎 108、横画 94。横画追得更快，对比下降。B 上碗开始收、下碗保持，腰轻收。M 谷加深，而不是简单加粗。字距略紧。

**600.** 调制降到 0.74，不再跟着 Regular 的粗细差走。接合与竖茎持平，开始给内白让路。B 腰明确内收，e 的开口被额外撑开，8 的下眼大于上眼。

**700.** 竖茎 150、接合只到 132，锐角内收形成轻微墨阱。字距比半粗回松。R 的腿缩短、裆部打开。和 zayJu 粗体相比仍更克制、更宽。

**800.** 调制 0.40，内白下限是 Regular 的 0.42。M 的谷重新抬起，免得黑三角封死。字距转为正值。B 上眼明显小于下眼，这是比例，不是均匀内缩。

**900.** 几乎单线（204/196），但 B 的上下碗、e 的开口、8 的上下眼仍不相等。接合 174 轻于茎。字距大幅放开。它不是特粗再加一圈。

## Oblique

Each Oblique is a 10° shear of **that weight’s own master**. Inner contours are sheared less (`counter_shear_keep`), sidebearings gain that weight’s pad, and open letters (a, c, e, g, s and the review figures) get an aperture nudge. Kerning is scaled by an extra 0.94. Filename and typographic subfamily say Oblique. Name ID 2 still uses Italic / Bold Italic where Windows style linking requires the RIBBI set. That is not a separately drawn italic.

Regular Oblique does not retarget the approved drawing. Its slant corrections are LihuiT `counter_shear_keep` 0.06, pad 16, aperture 3; zayJu 0.08, 14, 5.

## Knots removed before integer export

The measured cubics are the masters. Some joins double back on themselves for a few units. That kink is invisible until coordinates are rounded onto the integer grid, and the TrueType quadratic conversion widens it into a self-crossing. Those revisits are cut, and the contour is resampled onto the same curve. On the review letters the resampled points stay within 1 UPM of the measured curve. This is not a buffer, not a stem delta, and not a new drawing.

Glyphs that already round cleanly keep their cubics. A scaled figure — superiors, inferiors, fractions, case punctuation — that still crosses after the 0.57 scale is stored as its own repaired outline of that transformed drawing, so the scale is not asked to invent a knot. Accented letters stay components.

Stored masters are compiled without overlap removal. The union step was putting the knots back. Regular 400 upright still uses the approved compiler, overlap removal included.

测量得到的曲线仍是母版。少数接合会折返几个单位，整数取整和 TrueType 二次曲线转换后才会自交。只剪掉这些折返，再把轮廓重采样回同一条曲线。审查字母上的新点离测量曲线不超过 1 UPM。这不是外扩，也不是新的笔画。已经能取整的字形保留三次曲线。上标、下标、分数和 case 标点若在 0.57 倍缩放后仍然交叉，就存成那次缩放结果的独立轮廓。带音符的字母仍是部件。非 Regular 母版编译时不做重叠消除，否则结会回来。Regular 400 正体仍走原来的批准编译。

## Shapes the measured pass would not keep

- `.notdef` on every non-Regular weight: the outer box stays, and the frame thickness follows the vertical/horizontal stem ratio. The measured pass was deleting the counter.
- `uni2014` (em dash) at weight 500 only: rebuilt as a rounded bar whose thickness follows the horizontal stem ratio, advance kept at 1000. Other weights already took the measured pass.
- zayJu `uni238B` at Bold: the circle, its counter and the two arrow cutouts are offset separately so the cutouts stay. An elliptical union of the whole glyph had collapsed them. This symbol is not a review letter.

Superiors, inferiors and fractions remain scaled components of the same weight’s figures. They are not a separate optical-size master.

## What this document does not do

It does not approve the drawings. A human still has to look at B, M, R, a, g, e and 2/3/6/8/9 across the ladder, upright and Oblique, before v0.500 is published. It does not add Greek. U+03A3 without U+03C3 stays the known FontBakery failure.
